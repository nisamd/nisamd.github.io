I'm Nima Samadi, a researcher and software developer interested in AI, Embedded Systems, and Computers.

I enjoy understanding how ideas turn into working systems. My academic projects have involved computation offloading, embedded machine learning, and inference acceleration. Here, I want to write about what I learn along the way, including the details that are easy to forget.

Beyond research, this blog has room for Linux, networking, hardware experiments, books, and occasional personal writing.

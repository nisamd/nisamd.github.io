Welcome to my blog! I've been thinking about starting a blog for a while, and I finally think now is a good time. I looked at a few options and some blogs I like, and used them as inspiration. My main design reference is [Drew Silcock's blog](https://drew.silcock.dev/). It has great features, and I added many of them to this Hugo-based blog. A few, like showing how many people are reading right now, need a server, but those aren't important for now. I just want the blog to be fast, minimal, and easy to read. As they say, function over form 😁.

I have some ideas for posts, though I'm not sure I'll have time for all of them:

- My home server setup and the services I run
- Projects and research ideas I'm working on
- Things I run into at work
- Useful resources
- Occasional fun stuff!

I had a blog back in high school, where I mostly shared study material. I hosted it on Blogsky, but it's been years since I last updated it. This could be a worthy successor to that blog.
